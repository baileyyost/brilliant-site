Platform first. AI forward.

We rebuild the workflows behind known operating problems.

A workflow is the chain of people, decisions, systems and data that turns work into a result. Many were built for a different scale or an earlier generation of technology. We learn how yours runs, prepare the data and process underneath it, and build a working solution from the right combination of existing systems, integrations, automation, AI and custom interfaces.

Platform first. AI forward.

Platform first

The right operational foundation: connected systems, trustworthy data, clear workflows, business rules and an interface people can actually use. We assess the technology you already own and determine what needs to be improved, connected or built.

It doesn't mean choosing technology before we understand the problem, or asking you to buy a new platform.

AI forward

We actively look for where AI can improve the solution, and design the foundation so those capabilities can evolve.

Much of the work is better use of platforms, integrations and automation. AI expands what's possible as one part of the solution, applied where it makes a measurable difference.

Rules for predictable tasks.

Thresholds, routing and matching. Where the answer is knowable, a rule is faster, cheaper and consistent.

AI where interpretation or synthesis adds value.

Reading documents, summarizing cases, explaining variances. Each use is evaluated, and a person can review and correct it.

People where judgment and accountability matter.

The system prepares the decision and a person makes it.

What you receive.

A working product.

The interface the people doing the work actually use: the queue, the review screen, the decision support. Built around how the operation runs.

The connections behind it.

Your systems, data, rules, automation and AI working together, with access and controls your enterprise can govern.

A measured result.

Performance tracked against a baseline agreed with you, so you can see what changed.

Three ways to work with us.

Each engagement leads into the next. You can start with any of them, and most relationships move through all three, then begin again with the next workflow.

01

Understand & Scope

For when the problem is clear but the cause, the impact or the solution is not.

  • Map the workflow as it actually runs
  • Identify root causes
  • Assess data and systems
  • Establish a performance baseline
  • Recommend a solution with a defined implementation scope
You leave with

A baseline, a recommended solution and a defined scope for building it.

02

Build & Launch

For putting the recommended solution into use.

  • Build the interface, integrations, rules, automations and AI the workflow needs
  • Test with real users and real scenarios
  • Build in approvals and exception handling
  • Support rollout and adoption
You leave with

A working solution, in use by the people who do the work.

03

Operate & Improve

For keeping the solution performing once it is live.

  • Monitor performance against the baseline
  • Resolve issues and maintain integrations
  • Evaluate AI reliability
  • Track operating costs
  • Make agreed improvements
You leave with

A solution that keeps performing, with a clear view of what it costs and delivers.

Featured · Legal

Legal intake, contract review and approvals.

Problem

Requests arrive by email in every format. Routine agreements wait in the same queue as complex ones, and work goes to outside counsel because there is no clear way to triage it or manage the spend.

What we build

Structured requests, review against approved playbooks, AI-assisted review of key terms, and clear escalation to internal or external counsel. Counsel keeps the legal judgment.

What could improve

Faster turnaround on routine agreements, more consistent review, and less unnecessary outside counsel spend.

Finance close and reconciliation exceptions

Problem

Reconciliation exceptions surface late and are worked in spreadsheets, so the close runs long.

What we build

Matching rules for routine items, an exceptions queue with supporting detail, AI-assisted variance explanations, and review and sign-off built in.

What could improve

Earlier visibility of exceptions, fewer manual reconciliations and a more predictable close.

Supply-chain exceptions across disconnected systems

Problem

Orders, inventory and carrier data sit in separate systems that disagree. Planners spend the day finding problems.

What we build

One exceptions queue fed by each system, rules for routine mismatches and recommended actions for planners.

What could improve

Problems found sooner, fewer missed shipments and more time spent resolving them.

Renewals and cross-team handoffs

Problem

Account context is scattered across CRM, support and email, so renewals and handoffs between teams slip.

What we build

Shared ownership, status and due dates across teams, with AI-assisted account summaries and early warning on risk.

What could improve

Fewer surprises at renewal and fewer handoffs lost between teams.

Processes that have outgrown spreadsheets

Problem

A critical process runs on spreadsheets and shared drives, with no clear owner, history or controls.

What we build

A system of record with defined ownership, approvals, audit history and an interface built for the team.

What could improve

Less rework, clearer accountability and a process that can scale.
