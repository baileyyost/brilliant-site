We rebuild the workflow behind your biggest operating problem.

A workflow is the chain of people, decisions, systems, data and exceptions that turns work into a result. Behind almost every problem an operator has lived with for years, there is one of these chains that was built for a different scale or a different era of technology. Brilliant takes the workflow behind a problem you already know, redesigns it, builds it on the systems you already run, and delivers it as a working product the people doing the work will actually use.

Three things you can point to.

A working product.

An interface for the people doing the work — the queue, the review screen, the decision support — built around how the operation actually runs. Not a dashboard. Not a slide.

The connections behind it.

Your systems of record wired to the workflow layer, with the AI the process needs sitting inside it, and controls the enterprise can govern.

A number that is measured.

The result reported against the baseline you agreed to, every quarter, by us.
