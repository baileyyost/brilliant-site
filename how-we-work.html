How we work

One accountable lead. The team the problem requires.

You don't need a scoped project or a requirements document to begin. An Outcome FDE leads the work with the builders and specialists your problem needs, alongside your operating team and IT.

Who does the work.

The Outcome FDE.

A forward-deployed engineer accountable for the business outcome across the complete workflow. They learn how the work happens, guide the design and architecture, coordinate the build, and stay accountable for adoption and performance after launch. No one person is expected to be the expert in everything, so the right specialists work alongside them.

Builders and specialists.

Engineers, workflow specialists and AI specialists, with experts in the platforms you already run, such as SAP, OneStream, Salesforce or Airtable.

Your operating team.

The process owner who brought the problem and the people who do the work. They are part of the build, not just interviewed for it.

Your IT.

Involved from the start on architecture, security, access and systems of record, so the solution fits how you govern technology.

Start with one meaningful operating problem.

We choose a problem that matters to the business and can be measured. We map how the work runs today, with the people who do it.

Then we address what the solution depends on, such as data quality, process rules and ownership, and agree the baseline. This preparation is part of our work.

Build the solution and put it into use.

We build the interface, integrations, rules, automation and AI the workflow needs.

Working versions are tested with real users and real scenarios, including exceptions and failed actions. We support rollout and adoption with your team.

Measure and improve performance.

We track results against the baseline, along with cost, reliability and adoption.

We resolve issues, maintain integrations, evaluate AI reliability and make agreed improvements.

Extend to adjacent workflows.

The integrations, controls and knowledge already in place carry into the next problem, so each new workflow starts further ahead.

We continue to support what is live.
