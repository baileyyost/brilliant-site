We work out what the work needs. Then we build it.

Brilliant is an honest broker for improving business workflows. We architect and implement the right combination of your existing technology, integrations, automation and AI, and recommend replacing a system only when that is the best path for the business. You do not need a scoped workflow or a requirements document. We help define the problem and prepare the foundation with you.

Understand the work and prepare the foundation.

We work alongside your team to map the workflow as it actually runs: the systems, handoffs, decisions and exceptions. Together we agree on the scope and how success will be measured.

Then we address what the solution depends on: data quality, process rules and ownership. Reliable automation and agents need trustworthy information and a correctly designed workflow. This preparation is part of our work, scoped to the solution.

Architect the right solution.

We act as your honest broker, assessing the capabilities you already own before recommending additional technology.

We determine what belongs in your existing systems, what needs connecting, where conventional automation is sufficient, where AI adds value, and where people retain judgment or approval.

Options are compared on effectiveness, reliability, implementation effort and total operating cost, including licenses, AI usage and maintenance. We explain the tradeoffs and any relevant commercial relationships.

Build with the right experts.

The Outcome FDE leads delivery alongside workflow specialists, engineers, AI specialists and experts in your existing technology.

We build the integrations, applications, automations and agents the solution requires, then test working versions with real users and real scenarios, including exceptions and failed actions. We support rollout and adoption with your team.

Prove it, then build on it.

After launch we monitor results, cost, reliability and adoption, resolve issues and keep improving the workflow.

Once it runs reliably and delivers value, we move to the next workflow together, reusing the data, integrations, infrastructure and knowledge already in place while continuing to support what is live.

One person accountable for the outcome. The right experts beside them.

Forward-deployed engineers are part of our model. What shapes it is the Outcome FDE, supported by the expertise each solution needs, so no one person is expected to be the expert in everything.

The Outcome FDE.

A forward-deployed engineer responsible for the business outcome across the complete workflow. They understand how the work happens, guide the redesign and architecture, coordinate implementation, and remain accountable for adoption and operational performance after launch.

Workflow specialists and FDEs.

Workflow specialists know how operational work is structured and where it breaks down. Forward-deployed engineers build the integrations, applications and automations inside your environment, working alongside your team.

AI and platform experts.

AI specialists design and evaluate where models and agents add value. Experts in the platforms you already run, such as SAP, OneStream, Salesforce or Airtable, make sure the solution works with them as intended.

Your people.

The process owner who brought the problem, someone from IT, and the people who do the work. They are on the build, not interviewed for it.
