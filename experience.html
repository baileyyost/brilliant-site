The team has spent years turning operating problems into working systems: the workflow that runs the process, the agents that handle the repetitive judgment inside it, and the interface the operator actually uses. A sample of the work.

Supply chain· Industrial manufacturer

Exceptions queue

Reconciles orders, inventory and carrier status into one working queue so planners spend the day resolving problems instead of finding them. Rules handle the routine mismatches; people handle the ones that need judgment.

Systems: ERP, warehouse management, carrier feeds
Finance· Multi-entity company

Close and consolidation

Redesigned the monthly close around the controller's calendar: intercompany matching, reconciliations and variance review moved into one workflow with the review steps and sign-offs built in.

Systems: ERP, consolidation platform, spreadsheets retired
Legal· Consumer company

Contract intake and routing

Captures the review judgment as rules: standard terms proceed, deviations route to the right reviewer with the deviation highlighted. The legal team reads only what needs reading.

Systems: Contract repository, e-signature, email
Product· Software company

Customer ask intelligence

Systems: Support desk, CRM, product backlog
Operations· Retail company

Cross-team handoff tracking

Replaced the spreadsheet that tracked work moving between teams with a real system of record: ownership, status, due dates, and the handoffs that used to fall through the gap.

Moved pricing and quoting decisions out of spreadsheets into a workflow with the pricing rules encoded, approval thresholds enforced, and exceptions escalated with context.

Systems: CRM, pricing tables, quoting tools

Agents where they earn it. Rules where they don't. People where it matters.

Agents for repetitive judgment.

Where a person is making the same kind of call hundreds of times a day and a mistake is recoverable, an agent does it and a person reviews the exceptions.

Rules for deterministic logic.

Where the answer is knowable (thresholds, routing, matching) we write the rule. It is faster, cheaper and it never hallucinates.

People for the decisions that matter.

Where the call carries real consequence, the system prepares the decision and a human makes it.

Evaluation and review from day one.

Every agent ships with a way to measure whether it is right, and a path for a person to catch it when it isn't.

The technology has changed. The work hasn't.

For two decades the team has solved operating and finance problems inside the world's largest enterprises. The platforms underneath that work have changed. The job has not: understand the operation, build the system that runs it, stay accountable for the result.

Oracle

At MarketSphere, the team ran one of Oracle's largest enterprise performance management partners in North America. The work: financial consolidation, planning and forecasting, management reporting and close processes, rebuilt on Oracle for Fortune 500 finance organizations.

OneStream

At Black Diamond, the team helped large enterprises replace fragmented finance systems with OneStream's unified platform: consolidation, planning, account reconciliation and reporting in one place, delivered to global companies across North America and Europe.

Airtable

At Airtable, the team took the same discipline outside finance: custom workflow products for legal, product, marketing, operations and supply chain teams inside some of the largest enterprises in the world.
