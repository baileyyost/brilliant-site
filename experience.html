Experience

Two decades solving operating problems inside the enterprise.

The platforms have changed. The job has not: understand the operation, build the system that runs it and stay accountable for the result. The experience on this page was gained by the team at other organizations, before Brilliant.

Before Brilliant · Other organizations

Working alongside your vendors and partners.

Operating problems rarely sit inside one system. When yours spans several, we work with the software vendors and services partners already involved: agreeing who owns each part, coordinating changes across systems and keeping the workflow working from end to end.

We recommend adding a vendor only when it's the better option, and we tell you about any relevant commercial relationships.

Bring us the problem you know best.

You don't need to know the exact cost, the owner or the technical answer yet. The first conversation is about understanding the problem: where work is slow or costly, who it affects and which systems are involved. From there, we'll recommend the right next step.
