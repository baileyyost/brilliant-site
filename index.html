Three ways to work with us.

You can start with any of the three. Most relationships move through them in order, then begin again with the next workflow.

01

Understand & Scope

Map the workflow, identify root causes, assess data and systems, establish a baseline, and recommend a solution with a defined implementation scope.

02

Build & Launch

Build the interface, integrations, rules, automations and AI the workflow needs. Includes testing, approvals, exception handling, rollout and adoption.

03

Operate & Improve

Monitor performance, resolve issues, maintain integrations, evaluate AI reliability, track operating costs and make agreed improvements.
